# Content publishing cadence

Owner: Indian Infotech marketing, with technical review where required.

## Minimum cadence

- Publish two evidence-reviewed posts each month: one in the first half and one in the second half.
- Mix technical explainers, industry guides, product announcements, approved customer-success stories, and company news.
- Every article must include a named author or accountable editorial team, publication date, descriptive title and summary, relevant internal links, and related posts.
- Review product facts, customer claims, certifications, and compliance language with the responsible owner before publication.
- Do not publish client identities, outcomes, quotations, deployment metrics, certificates, awards, or partner claims without the source and public-display permission recorded in the evidence register.

## Monthly review

Check the editorial queue, expired or changed claims, broken links, resource requests, search performance, and the next two publication dates. Publishing frequency may increase only when the evidence and review workflow can sustain it.
