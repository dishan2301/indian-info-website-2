FAQs

Plan your setup
with clear answers.

Understand the device-to-attendance workflow and discuss your deployment requirements with our team.

How does device attendance become a daily record?

Employees punch on a biometric device. The data syncs into EasyTime Online, where your shift and attendance policies turn it into daily attendance, late marks and overtime.

Which devices are shown here?

The device range featured on this page includes Indian-15L, Indian-35 and Mini Indian-30. Contact Indian Infotech to discuss your device requirements and confirm compatibility with any devices you already own.

Can I manage multiple offices or companies?

EasyTime Online brings attendance from multiple companies, offices and locations into one workspace. Share your employee, device and location counts with our team to confirm the right setup and capacity.

How does EasyTime support payroll?

Approved attendance, leave and overtime provide payroll-ready inputs. Discuss your payroll system and required formats with our team to confirm the appropriate export or integration approach.

What can employees see?

Through employee self-service, employees can review their own attendance, working hours, shifts, leave and request status.
